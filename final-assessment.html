LEVEL 1 · FINAL ASSESSMENT

Final Conversation Assessment

Show what you can do in practical, real-life English. This is not a perfect-grammar test. The goal is to understand, respond, ask questions, and keep a conversation going.

How It Works

Complete three conversation scenarios: one everyday-life scenario, one community scenario, and one workplace scenario. Your teacher or conversation partner responds naturally and asks follow-up questions.

Remember: You may ask someone to repeat, slow down, explain, or clarify. Those are successful communication strategies.
SCENARIO A · EVERYDAY LIFE

🛒 Shopping & Schedule

You need to buy several things after work. Ask when the store closes, ask where two items are, ask the price of one item, and explain what time you finish work.

Partner: Ask, “How will you get to the store?”

SCENARIO B · COMMUNITY

📅 Appointment & Directions

Make an appointment. Confirm the day and time. Ask where the office is and how to get there. Ask for repetition if you need it.

Partner: Tell the student one appointment time is unavailable and offer another.

SCENARIO C · WORK

🤝 A New Workday

Introduce yourself to a coworker. Describe your job and schedule. Ask what you should do, clarify one instruction, and offer to help with another task.

Partner: Give a two-step instruction and then ask what time the student finishes.

OPTIONAL CHALLENGE

💬 Keep It Going

Have a two-minute conversation with no script. Ask at least two follow-up questions and use one clarification strategy.
