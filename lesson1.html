Introducing Yourself

🎯 Today's Goal / Meta de hoy

Introduce yourself, say where you are from, where you live and work, and ask another person the same questions.

Useful Phrases / Frases útiles

What's your name?¿Cómo te llamas?
My name is Maria.Me llamo María.
Where are you from?¿De dónde eres?
I'm from Mexico.Soy de México.
Where do you live?¿Dónde vives?
I live in Utah.Vivo en Utah.
Where do you work?¿Dónde trabajas?
I work at a restaurant.Trabajo en un restaurante.
Nice to meet you.Mucho gusto.

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

English usually needs a subject. Say “I work in a restaurant,” not simply “Work in a restaurant.”

✏️ Build Your English

Say each useful phrase aloud three times. Then create a true sentence about yourself using the new words.

Don't only translate. Practice saying the complete English sentence until it feels natural.

💬 Read It → Speak It

A: Hi! My name is Carlos. What's your name?

B: My name is Ana.

A: Nice to meet you. Where are you from?

B: I'm from Mexico. I live in Utah.

Practice once while reading. Then look away and have the conversation again in your own words.

🎯 Real-Life Challenge

Have a one-minute conversation using today's language with another person. Try it with three people.
