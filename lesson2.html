My Family

Mi familia

0% complete

🎯 Today's Goal / Meta de hoy

Talk about the people in your family, say who they are, and ask someone simple questions about their family.

Useful Phrases / Frases útiles

This is my family.Esta es mi familia.
This is my husband.Este es mi esposo.
This is my wife.Esta es mi esposa.
I have two children.Tengo dos hijos.
I have one son and one daughter.Tengo un hijo y una hija.
Do you have children?¿Tienes hijos?
Yes, I do.Sí.
No, I don't.No.
How many children do you have?¿Cuántos hijos tienes?

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

In English, “parents” means mother and father. “Relatives” is the broader word for familiares. Also remember: “I have two children,” not “I have two childs.”

A: How many sons and daughters do you have?
