My Family

Mi familia

0% complete

🎯 Goal

Talk simply about your family.

Useful Phrases

This is my family.Esta es mi familia.
I have two children.Tengo dos hijos.
Do you have children?¿Tienes hijos?
I have one son.Tengo un hijo.
I have one daughter.Tengo una hija.

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

Children is the plural of child: one child, two children.

Vocabulary

✏️ Practice

  1. Name five family members.
  2. Say how many children you have, if any.
  3. Ask a partner about brothers or sisters.
Say your answers aloud. Speaking is the goal.

💬 Conversation

A: Do you have children?

B: Yes. I have two children.

A: Do you have brothers or sisters?

B: I have two sisters.
