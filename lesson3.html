LESSON 3

Numbers, Age & Phone Numbers

Números, edad y teléfonos

0% complete

🎯 Goal

Say numbers, age, and phone numbers and ask for repetition.

Useful Phrases

How old are you?¿Cuántos años tienes?
I'm 35 years old.Tengo 35 años.
What's your phone number?¿Cuál es tu número?
Can you repeat that?¿Puedes repetir eso?
Please say it slowly.Por favor, dilo despacio.

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

For age, English uses BE: “I'm 35,” not “I have 35 years.”

✏️ Practice

  1. Count 0–20.
  2. Contrast 13/30, 14/40, 15/50.
  3. Read a pretend phone number one digit at a time.
  4. Ask a partner to repeat a number.
Say your answers aloud. Speaking is the goal.

💬 Conversation

A: What's your phone number?

B: It's 801-555-2479.

A: Can you repeat that slowly?

B: 8-0-1, 5-5-5, 2-4-7-9.

🎯 Real-Life Challenge

Give a phone number and have your partner repeat it back.
