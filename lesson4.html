LESSON 4

My Day

Mi día

0% complete

🎯 Goal

Describe your daily routine and ask about another person's day.

Useful Phrases

I wake up at 6:00.Me despierto a las 6:00.
I eat breakfast.Desayuno.
I go to work.Voy al trabajo.
I go home after work.Voy a casa después del trabajo.
I go to bed at 10:00.Me acuesto a las 10:00.

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

Use simple present for routines: “I work every day.” With she/he: “She works.” Say “I go home,” not “I go to home.”

Vocabulary

✏️ Practice

  1. Put routine actions in order.
  2. Say five sentences about your day.
  3. Ask: What time do you wake up?
  4. Describe what you do after work.
Say your answers aloud. Speaking is the goal.

💬 Conversation

A: What time do you wake up?

B: I wake up at 6:30.

A: What do you do after work?

B: I go home and make dinner.

🎯 Real-Life Challenge

Talk about your day for one minute using at least five actions.
