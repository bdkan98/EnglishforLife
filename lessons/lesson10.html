My Job

Mi trabajo

0% completeListen • Practice • Speak • Use It

🎯 Today's Goal

Describe your job, workplace, schedule, and basic responsibilities.

🔊 Useful Phrases

What do you do for work?¿En qué trabajas?
I work at a restaurant.Trabajo en un restaurante.
I'm a manager.Soy gerente.
I start at eight and finish at four.Empiezo a las ocho y termino a las cuatro.
I'm responsible for helping customers.Soy responsable de ayudar a los clientes.

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

Spanish often uses soy with a profession. In English, most singular jobs need a/an: “I'm a manager.” “I'm an electrician.”
