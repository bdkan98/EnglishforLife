Talking to Coworkers

Hablar con compañeros de trabajo

🎯 Today's Goal

Ask for help, clarify instructions, communicate a problem, and offer help at work.

🔊 Useful Phrases

Can you help me, please?¿Puedes ayudarme, por favor?
What do you need me to do?¿Qué necesitas que haga?
Could you show me one more time?¿Podrías mostrarme una vez más?
I'm finished. What should I do next?Terminé. ¿Qué debo hacer ahora?
I'll take care of it.Yo me encargo.

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

At work, polite questions such as Can you…? and Could you…? usually sound more cooperative than very short commands.

🤝 What Would You Say?

You don't understand an instruction.
You finished your task.
Your coworker is busy and you can help.

💬 Workplace Problem-Solving

Situation 1: You were given instructions, but you are not sure what to do. Ask your coworker to explain or demonstrate again.

Situation 2: You notice your coworker needs help. Offer help and decide who will do each task.

🎯 Live It

Have a one-minute work conversation without reading. Ask for help, clarify one detail, and finish by confirming what you will do.
