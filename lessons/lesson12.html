Combine Level 1 skills in longer, unscripted everyday conversations.

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

Real conversation is not about speaking perfectly. Use strategies to keep the conversation going: ask someone to repeat, confirm details, and use words you already know.

🎧 Listen & Respond

Without replaying immediately, tell a partner everything you remember. Then listen again and add missing details.

🧠 Conversation Strategies

Could you repeat that, please?Ask for repetition.
Let me make sure I understand.Confirm information.
Do you mean ___?Clarify a detail.

💬 Three Conversation Rounds

Round 1 · Community: Ask for directions to a store, ask when it closes, and buy an item.

Round 2 · Appointment: Make an appointment, confirm the date/time, and ask for directions.

Round 3 · Work: Introduce yourself to a coworker, discuss your schedule, ask for help, and clarify a task.

🎯 No-Script Challenge

Talk with a partner for two minutes without reading. If you get stuck, use a conversation strategy instead of switching languages immediately.
