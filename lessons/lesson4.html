LESSON 4

My Day

Mi día

0% completeListen • Practice • Speak

🎯 Today's Goal

Describe your daily routine and ask another person about their day.

🔊 Useful Phrases

Tap the speaker and repeat each phrase.

I wake up at 6:00.Me despierto a las 6:00.
I eat breakfast.Desayuno.
I go to work.Voy al trabajo.
I go home after work.Voy a casa después del trabajo.
I go to bed at 10:00.Me acuesto a las 10:00.

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

Use simple present for routines: “I work every day.” With he/she: “She works.” Say “I go home,” not “I go to home.”
