Time & Schedules

La hora y los horarios

0% completeListen • Practice • Speak

🎯 Today's Goal

Ask and answer questions about time, work schedules, and appointments.

🔊 Useful Phrases

What time is it?¿Qué hora es?
It's seven thirty.Son las siete y media.
What time do you start work?¿A qué hora empiezas a trabajar?
I start work at eight.Empiezo a trabajar a las ocho.
I'm available after three.Estoy disponible después de las tres.

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

For clock time, English uses at: “I start at 8:00.” Use in with parts of the day: “in the morning,” but say “at night.”

📅 Build a Schedule

Complete the sentences aloud with your real information:

  • I wake up at ______.
  • I start work/class at ______.
  • I eat lunch at ______.
  • I go home at ______.
  • I go to bed at ______.

💬 Schedule Conversation

A: What time do you start work?

B: I start at eight.

A: What time do you finish?

B: I finish at four thirty.

A: Are you available after five?

B: Yes, I am.

Round 1: Read it. Round 2: Use your real schedule.

🎯 Live It

Ask a partner three questions about their schedule. Then tell the class one thing you learned about your partner.
