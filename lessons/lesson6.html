Food & Shopping

Comida y compras

0% completeListen • Practice • Speak

🎯 Today's Goal

Ask for food, understand prices, quantities, and complete a simple purchase.

🔊 Useful Phrases

How much is this?¿Cuánto cuesta esto?
I'd like two, please.Quisiera dos, por favor.
Do you have any milk?¿Tiene leche?
Where can I find the bread?¿Dónde puedo encontrar el pan?
That's all, thank you.Eso es todo, gracias.

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

How much? asks about price or an uncountable amount. How many? asks about countable things: “How many apples?”

🧩 Choose the Polite Request

💬 Role Play: At the Store

Customer: Ask where an item is. Ask the price. Buy a quantity. Ask for a bag or receipt.

Worker: Tell the customer where the item is, give a price, and complete the purchase.

Switch roles after two minutes.

🎯 Live It

Create a pretend shopping list with five items. Ask your partner where to find three items and how much two items cost.
