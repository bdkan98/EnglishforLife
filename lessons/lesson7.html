Directions & Places

Direcciones y lugares

0% completeListen • Practice • Speak

🎯 Today's Goal

Ask where a place is, understand simple directions, and give basic directions.

🔊 Useful Phrases

Excuse me, where is the pharmacy?Disculpe, ¿dónde está la farmacia?
Go straight.Siga derecho.
Turn left at the light.Gire a la izquierda en el semáforo.
It's next to the bank.Está al lado del banco.
Is it far from here?¿Está lejos de aquí?

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

Use Where is…? for location. “Where is the bank?” For directions, English often uses short commands: “Go straight.” “Turn right.”

👣 Follow the Directions

Say these aloud and point in the correct direction:

  1. Go straight.
  2. Turn right.
  3. Turn left at the light.
  4. It's next to the bank.

💬 Ask for Directions

A: Excuse me, where is the pharmacy?

B: Go straight and turn right at the light.

A: Is it far from here?

B: No. It's next to the bank.

A: Thank you!

🎯 Live It

Choose a real place you know. Give your partner three directions to get there. Your partner should ask at least one question.
