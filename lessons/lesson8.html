Transportation

Transporte

0% completeListen • Practice • Speak

🎯 Today's Goal

Talk about how you travel, ask about buses or trains, and understand simple transportation information.

🔊 Useful Phrases

How do you get to work?¿Cómo llegas al trabajo?
I drive to work.Voy al trabajo en carro.
Which bus do I take?¿Qué autobús tomo?
Where is the bus stop?¿Dónde está la parada de autobús?
What time does the bus arrive?¿A qué hora llega el autobús?

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

Use by for many transportation methods: “by bus,” “by train,” “by car.” But say “I drive,” “I walk,” or “I take the bus.”

💬 Transportation Conversation

A: How do you get to work?

B: I take the bus.

A: What time does your bus arrive?

B: At seven fifteen.

A: Where is the bus stop?

B: It's across from the market.

🎯 Live It

Tell a partner how you normally travel to work, school, the store, or another place. Ask your partner three transportation questions.
