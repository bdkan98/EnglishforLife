🎯 Today's Goal

Make, confirm, change, and understand a simple appointment.

🔊 Useful Phrases

I'd like to make an appointment.Quisiera hacer una cita.
What days are available?¿Qué días están disponibles?
Does Tuesday at ten work?¿Le sirve el martes a las diez?
I need to reschedule my appointment.Necesito cambiar mi cita.
Could you repeat that, please?¿Podría repetir eso, por favor?

🇪🇸 Spanish-Speaker Tip

English often uses make an appointment, not “take an appointment.” For a scheduled time, use at: “Tuesday at ten.”

🎧 Listen for Details

💬 Make an Appointment

A: How can I help you?

B: I'd like to make an appointment.

A: Does Wednesday at two thirty work?

B: Yes, that works. Thank you.

🎯 Live It

Partner A is the receptionist. Partner B needs an appointment. Choose a day and time, confirm it, then switch roles. In round two, reschedule the appointment.
