CHECKPOINT 2 · LESSONS 5–8

Use your English in real situations.

Review schedules, shopping, directions, and transportation. This checkpoint includes listening, practical understanding, and speaking.

1 · Listening: A Morning Schedule

Listen twice if needed. Try not to read anything while you listen.

What time does the speaker start work?
How does the speaker get to work?
Where does the speaker go after work?

2 · Real-Life English

You want to know the price. What do you say?
You need the pharmacy. What do you ask?
Someone says, “Turn right at the light.” What should you do?
You travel on a bus. Which sentence sounds natural?

3 · Speaking Check

Complete these with a partner or teacher. Check each one after you can do it without reading a model answer.

4 · Conversation Challenge

Situation: You need to go to a store after work. Ask your partner what time the store closes, how to get there, and where it is. Then pretend to buy one item and ask the price.

Teacher/partner: answer naturally and ask one follow-up question.
