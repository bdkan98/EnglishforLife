Conversation Cards · Lessons 1–12

Pair students, switch roles, then repeat without reading.

1 · Meet Me

What's your name?
Where are you from?
Where do you live?
Where do you work?

2 · Family

Do you have children?
Brothers or sisters?
Tell me about your family.

3 · Numbers

How old are you?
Say a pretend phone number.
Ask your partner to repeat it.

4 · My Day

When do you wake up?
What do you do in the morning?
What do you do after work?

5 · My Schedule

What time do you wake up?
When do you start work?
When are you available?

6 · At the Store

Where can I find ___?
How much is it?
I'd like ___, please.

7 · Directions

Where is the ___?
Is it far?
Give three directions.

8 · Transportation

How do you get to work?
Where is the bus stop?
What time does it arrive?

9 · Appointments

I'd like to make an appointment.
What days are available?
I need to reschedule.

10 · My Job

What do you do for work?
What's your schedule?
What are you responsible for?

11 · Coworkers

Can you help me?
Could you show me again?
What should I do next?

12 · Conversation Challenge

Ask a follow-up question.
Clarify something.
Keep talking for two minutes.
