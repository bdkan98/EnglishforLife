Conversation Cards · Lessons 1–4

Pair students, switch roles, then repeat without reading.

1 · Meet Me

What's your name?
Where are you from?
Where do you live?
Where do you work?

2 · Family

Do you have children?
Brothers or sisters?
Tell me about your family.

3 · Numbers

How old are you?
Say a pretend phone number.
Ask your partner to repeat it.

4 · My Day

When do you wake up?
What do you do in the morning?
What do you do after work?
