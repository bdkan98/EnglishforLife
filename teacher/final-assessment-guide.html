LEVEL 1 · FINAL

Final Conversation Assessment Guide

Evaluate whether the learner can communicate independently in practical Level 1 situations.

Administration

Complete Scenarios A, B, and C from the student assessment. Avoid correcting the student during the conversation unless communication breaks down. Ask natural follow-up questions. Allow the student to request repetition or clarification.

Speaking Rubric · 20 Points

IndependenceMaintains conversation with minimal support.Completes tasks with limited support.Needs regular prompts/models.Depends heavily on scripts or translation.

Suggested completion standard: 14/20 or higher, with no category at zero. A learner below the standard should receive targeted practice and reassessment rather than being treated as having “failed” the course.

Teacher comments:

Assessment Principle

Accent is not a scoring category. Minor grammar errors should not lower a score when the learner's meaning is clear. Score the learner's ability to accomplish the communication task.
