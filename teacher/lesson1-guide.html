Lesson 1 Teacher Guide

Objective: Introduce themselves and answer five personal questions.

60–75 Minute Flow

  1. Warm-up/review — 5 minutes
  2. Model vocabulary and phrases — 15 minutes
  3. Guided practice — 15 minutes
  4. Partner conversation — 15 minutes
  5. No-reading speaking challenge — 10 minutes
  6. Quick check — 10 minutes

Conversation Prompts

  • What's your name?
  • Where are you from?
  • Where do you live?
  • Where do you work?

Coaching

Let students finish before correcting. Prioritize understandable communication. Model the correct form naturally, have the student repeat it once, and keep the conversation moving.
