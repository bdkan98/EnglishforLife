UNIT 3 · 60–75 MINUTES

Lesson 12 · Conversation Challenge

Objective: Students combine Level 1 language in longer unscripted conversations.

Suggested Flow

  1. Warm-up and prior-language review — 8 min
  2. Model useful phrases with Normal/Slow audio — 12 min
  3. Guided practice — 15 min
  4. Partner role-play — 15 min
  5. No-script challenge — 15 min
  6. Quick check/reflection — 10 min

Teaching Focus

Reduce written support across each round. The goal is communication repair, follow-up questions, and confidence—not memorization.

Coaching

Correct errors that block understanding first. Recast smaller grammar errors naturally and keep the conversation moving. Encourage students to use clarification strategies.
