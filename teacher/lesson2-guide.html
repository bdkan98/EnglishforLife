60–75 MINUTES

My Family

Objective: Identify and talk simply about family members.

Suggested Flow

  1. Welcome & review — 5 min
  2. Model vocabulary and phrases — 15 min
  3. Interactive guided practice — 15 min
  4. Partner conversation — 15 min
  5. No-reading real-life challenge — 10 min
  6. Quick check — 10 min

Coaching Notes

Give students time to finish their thought. Prioritize understandable communication. Avoid correcting every small error. Model the natural English form and encourage another attempt.
