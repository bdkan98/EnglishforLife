60–75 MINUTES

Food & Shopping

Objective: Ask for items, understand prices and quantities, and complete a simple purchase.

Suggested Flow

  1. Warm-up and previous lesson review — 8 min
  2. Model new phrases with Normal/Slow audio — 12 min
  3. Guided interactive practice — 15 min
  4. Partner role-play — 20 min
  5. No-reading real-life challenge — 10 min
  6. Quick check — 10 min

Teaching Focus

Use real packages, grocery ads, or classroom objects when possible. Practice polite complete requests rather than isolated vocabulary.

Coaching

Prioritize successful communication. Recast errors naturally rather than interrupting every sentence. Encourage students to ask for repetition when they do not understand.
