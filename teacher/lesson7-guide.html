60–75 MINUTES

Directions & Places

Objective: Ask for a location, understand simple directions, and give basic directions.

Suggested Flow

  1. Warm-up/review — 8 min
  2. Model new language with Normal/Slow audio — 12 min
  3. Guided visual/listening practice — 15 min
  4. Partner conversation — 15 min
  5. Real-life role-play — 15 min
  6. Quick check — 10 min

Teaching Focus

Use the classroom as a map. Place signs around the room and have students physically follow directions such as go straight, turn left, and next to.

Coaching

Keep the activity communicative. Allow gestures, maps, and clarification questions. The goal is for students to solve a real communication problem in English.
