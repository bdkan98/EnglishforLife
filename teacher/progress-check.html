Beginner Oral Progress Check

Assess understandable communication—not perfect grammar.

4 · IndependentResponds without help.
3 · DevelopingMinor prompting or repetition.
2 · EmergingNeeds models or significant prompting.
1 · BeginningCannot yet respond without reading/modeling.

Skills

  • Introduces self
  • Talks about family
  • Says age/phone numbers clearly
  • Describes five daily routine actions
  • Asks questions independently
  • Uses “Can you repeat that?”
