LESSONS 1–4

Review & Speaking Checkpoint

Use this after Lesson 4 before moving into the next unit.

Suggested 45-Minute Review

  1. Introductions in rotating pairs — 8 min
  2. Family conversation — 8 min
  3. Number and phone-number dictation — 8 min
  4. Daily routine speaking — 10 min
  5. Student website checkpoint — 10 min

Speaking Standard

A student is ready to continue when they can communicate the main idea without reading, even with grammar or pronunciation errors. Give extra practice when errors prevent understanding.

Teacher Tip

Ask follow-up questions rather than turning the checkpoint into a memorization test. The goal is flexible communication.
